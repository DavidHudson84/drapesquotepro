How to measure (tap to view)

Curtains

  • Width — measure the track from end to end (not the curtain hem). This works for standard tracks, rods, and S-fold tracks.
  • Height — measure from the top of the track down to where the curtain finishes (usually the floor). Easy trick: if your curtain touches the floor, measure floor to top of the track.
  • Can't measure? Just pick the closest height range — we use three tiers and any of them will get you a fair quote.

Blinds

  • Width — measure the base rail at the bottom of the blind, end to end.
  • Height — measure from the base rail up to the top of the blind.

Measure in metres (e.g. 2.4) or millimetres (e.g. 2400). Either works.
