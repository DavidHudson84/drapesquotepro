# Dr Drapes Partner Portal — quick guide

Use the portal to quote curtain and blind cleaning on the Dr Drapes price
list, record a pre-inspection, send the quote to your customer and follow the
job through to rehang. It works on a phone, a tablet or a computer.

**Portal:** https://quote.drdrapes.com.au/partner.html (worth bookmarking)
**Help:** Dr Drapes, 1300 652 866 · drdrapes@hudsongroup.com.au

---

## 1. Set up your login (once)

You don't sign up yourself. Dr Drapes sets up a login for each person in your
business.

1. Look for an email from **Dr Drapes Curtain Clinic** with the subject *Your
   Dr Drapes partner login*. Check your junk folder if it isn't there.
2. Click **Set your password** and choose one (at least 8 characters). The
   link works once and lasts 7 days.
3. You're in. From now on, sign in at the portal address above with your email
   and that password.

**Forgot your password, or the link expired?** On the sign-in page, click
*Forgot password, or never set one?*, enter your email, and we'll send you a fresh
link.

## 2. Create a quote

1. Press **+ New quote**.
2. **Customer.** Enter their name (required), phone, email and site address.
   Add your own job number in the reference field if you use one. It goes on
   the quote and on our invoice.
3. **Who does the takedown and rehang?**
   - *Dr Drapes*: we take down, clean and rehang. Our rehang charge is added
     to the quote.
   - *Your business*: you take down and rehang, we clean. You earn the higher
     commission rate.
4. **Who bills the customer?**
   - *Dr Drapes*: we invoice your customer and pay your commission monthly.
   - *Your business*: we invoice you at the quote price less your commission,
     and you bill your customer.
5. **Items.** Name the room, then for each window choose the item type and enter
   the **width** and **drop** (the drop isn't needed for pelmets, swags and
   valances). You can enter metres (2.4) or millimetres (2400). Tick **Mould
   treatment** if needed and add a note if it helps (for example "left window,
   pinch pleat").
   - **+ Curtain / blind** adds another item to the room.
   - **+ Repair / hardware** adds cords, hooks, track repairs and so on, with a
     quantity.
   - **+ Add another room** for the next room.
6. Watch the bar at the bottom: it shows the price ex GST, the total inc GST and
   **your commission**, updating as you type. Prices come straight from the
   Dr Drapes price list and can't be changed in the portal.
7. Add any **notes for the customer** (these print on the quote) and, for an
   insurance job, the insurer and claim number.
8. Press **Save quote**. It gets a quote number (DRQ-…) and opens.

You can edit a quote any time until the customer accepts it. Press **Edit
quote** on the job.

## 3. Send the quote to your customer

Open the job and choose one:

- **Email to customer** (recommended). Check the email address and message and
  press **Send**. Your customer gets the quote PDF with an **Accept online**
  button. You're copied in, and their replies come to you and to us. When they
  accept online, the job confirms itself and you're both told. Nothing else to do.
- **Download PDF.** Send it however you like. When the customer says yes, open
  the job and press **Customer has accepted**, then add anything we should know
  (access, timing, pets). If they say no, press **Customer declined**.

The quote carries the Dr Drapes branding, "In partnership with" your business
name and contact details, and our terms of trade.

## 4. Do a pre-inspection

Do this before anything is taken down. It records the condition of the
curtains and blinds, and protects you, your customer and us if there's a
question later.

1. Open the job and press **Start pre-inspection**.
2. Tick any conditions you can see (mould, fading, fraying, water marks, track
   damage and so on) and add a short note against each. Tick **None on this
   job** for blinds or curtains if there aren't any.
3. Set the overall condition score (1 poor to 10 excellent).
4. Add **notes for the customer** (these appear on the inspection report) and
   **notes for Dr Drapes** (access, fragile items, anything we should know).
5. Add up to **8 photos**. On a phone, this opens the camera.
6. Press **Save pre-inspection**.

You can come back and change it until the job reaches our workshop.

## 5. Follow the job

Your home screen lists every job, with tabs for **Open quotes**, **Accepted &
in progress**, **Completed** and **Declined**. It also shows your open
commission and what you've earned this financial year.

We email you automatically when a job:

- is accepted
- arrives in our workshop
- is cleaned and ready (if you're rehanging, that's your cue to arrange
  pickup)
- is invoiced, with your commission amount
- is completed and rehung
- is declined

Each email has a button that opens the job in the portal.

## 6. Commission

- Your rates are shown at the top of your home screen: one rate when Dr Drapes
  does the takedown and rehang, a higher one when you do.
- Commission is on the **cleaning and repair** amounts, **ex GST**. Transport,
  rehang and call-out charges aren't included.
- It's earned in the month the job is invoiced. On the 1st of each month you
  get a statement listing each job and the amount. If you're registered for GST,
  GST is added when we pay.
- On jobs where you bill the customer, your commission is already taken off our
  invoice to you, so nothing is paid separately.

## Tips

- Measure **track width** for curtains, and **width × drop** of the blind
  itself for blinds.
- Not sure what something is? Choose the closest item and add a note. We'll
  check it at takedown and adjust before any work starts if needed.
- A login is personal. Ask us for a separate one for each person in your team
  rather than sharing.
