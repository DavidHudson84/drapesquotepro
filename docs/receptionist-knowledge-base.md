## About us
Dr Drapes Curtain Clinic cleans curtains, drapes and blinds across Melbourne and
regional Victoria. We've been operating since 1983 and are part of the Hudson's
Dry Cleaning Group (Master Dry Cleaners). Our clinic is at 2/61 Reserve Rd, Melton.

Phone 1300 652 866. Email drdrapes@hudsongroup.com.au.

## Office hours
The office is staffed Monday to Friday, 9am to 2:30pm. Outside those hours, take a
message, and the team will call back the next business day.

## Service area
We cover the whole Melbourne metro area and some regional areas, from Williamstown
to Warranwood and Sunbury to Mornington, plus Geelong, Ballarat and the Mornington
Peninsula. We travel up to about two hours from Melton. Beyond that (for example
Bairnsdale or Gippsland), customers are welcome to bring their curtains to the
Melton clinic, and we'll clean them there.

Each area has set days that the van visits (for example, the Mornington Peninsula
is Wednesdays). The team books the take-down to suit the next run to that area.
Don't promise a particular day.

## How the service works
**Take down | Dry clean | Rehang.**

1. **Quote.** The customer fills in the online quote form with rough sizes and
   photos, or sends them by email. We send a written quote by email.
2. **Accept.** Accept online using the button in the quote email. We then confirm
   the full address and book a take-down time. Times are given as an arrival window,
   for example 1 to 3pm.
3. **Take-down.** Our technician takes the curtains or blinds down, and re-measures
   everything on site. The final price is based on those measurements.
4. **Cleaning.** Everything is cleaned off site at our Melton clinic, to the care
   label. It's a proper dry clean that gets between the face fabric and the lining,
   where dust, grime and insects collect.
5. **Rehang.** We bring everything back and rehang it.

Take-down and rehang are included in the price, and there's no separate labour
charge.

## Turnaround
Allow 2 to 3 weeks from take-down to rehang. Large jobs and busy periods can take a
little longer. A faster turnaround can sometimes be arranged. It's priced per job, so take a
message and Nicole will confirm what's possible and the cost.

**Temporary curtains:** we provide temporary drapes free of charge while yours are
being cleaned. Ask whether they'd like them, and note it in the message.

## How to measure
Rough sizes are fine. We re-measure everything on site, so customers don't need to
be exact.

- **Curtains:** the width of the track (or of each curtain), and the drop from the
  top of the curtain to the hem.
- **Blinds:** width left to right, and drop top to bottom.
- Metres or millimetres are both fine (2.4 or 2400).
- A photo of each window helps, plus close-ups of any stains, mould or damage.

## Venetian blinds and blind repairs
We usually refer venetian blind cleaning, and parts or mechanism repairs on blinds,
to a specialist partner. Don't promise either way. Take a message with their details,
suburb and what they have, and Nicole will either quote it or pass them on.

## Repairs and hardware
We can do most curtain repairs while the items are with us: hooks, rings, weights,
cords, hems, linings, headings and seams, as well as track repairs and track
replacement. Repairs are always optional, and agreed with the customer before the work is
done.

## Payment
We send the invoice once the cleaning is done. Payment is by bank transfer or credit
card over the phone. Payment is due before rehang, so the curtains stay with us
until the invoice is paid. Sally never takes card details. The team calls the
customer to take payment.

## Fabric condition — what to tell people
Curtains spend years in sun, heat and condensation, and that weakens fabric in ways
you often can't see until it's cleaned. We take every care and clean to the care
label, but we can't guarantee that old, sun-rotted or heavily stained fabric will
come up like new.

- If fabric is past saving, we'll say so at inspection, before any work is done.
- Australian Standard AS/NZS 1957 allows up to 3% shrinkage on professionally made
  curtains.
- Our terms of trade are sent with every quote.

If a caller is unhappy with a finished job, don't argue or explain. Take a detailed
message marked urgent, so the team can call them back.

## Common questions
- **Is it worth cleaning, or should I replace them?** Made-to-measure curtains are
  expensive to replace. Most of what people want gone (dust, smoke, odour, light
  mould) comes out with a proper clean. If the fabric is past saving, we'll tell you
  at inspection, before any work is done.
- **Can you steam clean them on the window?** No. We clean off site, to the care
  label, which is how fabrics and linings are designed to be cleaned. That's also
  how we can treat mould and stains properly. (The odd Roman blind can be done on
  site by arrangement. Take a message.)
- **I'm moving out. Can I get a receipt for my bond or agent?** Yes, we provide one.
- **Can I spread the cost?** The team can work with you. Doing the main living
  areas first, or splitting the job over two visits, are common ways to do it.
- **How often should curtains be cleaned?** About every five years, or more often
  in busy rooms or homes with wood heaters or open fires.
- **Do I need to be home?** Someone needs to let us in for the take-down and the
  rehang.
- **Do you do commercial work?** Yes: real estate agents, property managers, clubs,
  aged care and theatres. Take the business name, contact and site, and the team
  will quote.

## Insurance and restoration
We work with insurers, builders and property managers on fire, flood, smoke and
mould damage. Take the claim or work order number, the insurer or agency, the
property suburb, what's affected, and whether the damage is still happening. Mark
the message urgent if it is.
