## How to measure
[Confirm against what Sophie tells customers now.]
- **Width:** measure the width of the curtain panel itself, flat, not the window.
  For a pair, measure each panel.
- **Height (drop):** from the top of the curtain to the bottom hem.
- Metres or millimetres are both fine (2.4 or 2400).
- For blinds: width and drop of the blind.
- Take a photo of each window, and of any stains, mould or damage.
- Not sure? Rough sizes and photos are enough. We'll check on the day.

## Shrinkage and fabric condition
Curtains and blinds spend years in sunlight, heat and condensation, and that can
weaken fabric in ways you can't see until it's cleaned. Australian Standard AS/NZS
1957 allows up to 3% shrinkage on professionally made curtains when cleaned. Our
process keeps it to a minimum. Our full terms of trade come with every quote.
